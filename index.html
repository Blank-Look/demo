How to play

  • You are Black and move first. The bot plays White.
  • Place a stone on any empty intersection.
  • Surround a group’s last empty point to capture it.
  • Suicide and immediately recreating the previous position (ko) are illegal.
  • Pass twice in a row to end the game and score.
  • Undo reverses your move and the bot’s reply.

Arrow keys move around the board; Enter or Space plays.

Rules of 9×9 Go

Goal. Control more area than your opponent. You play Black; the bot plays White.

  • Stones. Players take turns placing one stone on an empty intersection. Black goes first.
  • Liberties. Empty points next to a stone or connected group are its liberties.
  • Capture. When a group has no liberties left, it is removed from the board.
  • Suicide. You may not place a stone that leaves its own group with no liberties unless the move captures.
  • Ko. You may not immediately recreate the position that existed before your opponent’s last move.
  • Passing. You may pass. Two passes in a row end the game.
  • Scoring. Area scoring: your stones plus empty points surrounded only by your colour. White receives 6.5 komi.

Dead stones are not removed automatically. If both sides disagree on the position, resign or continue play before passing.
